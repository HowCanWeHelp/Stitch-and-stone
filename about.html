About Stitch & Stone · Meet Jess

Tiny turtles.
Beautiful stones.
A whole lot of love.

From my worktable to your world — the story behind the stitches.

Hi, I’m Jess! The hands behind the hooks, the love behind the turtles, and the slightly chaotic human who probably has yarn somewhere it absolutely shouldn’t be.

Stitch & Stone started with a love for creating things by hand and grew into something so much bigger than I ever imagined. I make tiny crochet turtles with genuine gemstone shells, combining two things I love into little companions that are as unique as the people who adopt them.

Every turtle is handmade with love, one stitch at a time. No two stones are exactly alike, and no two turtles are ever quite the same. Each one has its own little personality, its own kind of magic, and a chance to become someone’s special little companion.

What started as a creative passion has grown into a community of turtle lovers, crystal enthusiasts, collectors, and the sweetest people who have welcomed these little guys into their homes. From local adoptions to turtles finding their forever homes across Canada and beyond, I’m still a little amazed by where this adventure has taken me.

Behind every turtle is a whole lot of love, a little bit of yarn, and a real human doing her best to turn a little idea into something meaningful. Stitch & Stone is about more than crochet or crystals. It’s about the joy of creating, the excitement of finding the one, and the little things that make an ordinary day feel a bit more special.

Whether you’re here for the gemstones, the tiny faces, the silly personalities, or because you simply needed a little turtle-shaped happiness in your life, I’m so glad you’re here.

Thank you for supporting handmade, for believing in small businesses, and for giving my little creations a place in your world. Every order, every kind message, every share, and every adoption means more to me than you know.

I hope your little turtle brings you a smile, a bit of comfort, and a little extra happiness whenever you need it most.

With love, yarn, and probably a few unfinished projects,

Jess
Founder & Turtle Lady, Stitch & Stone

And as always, I hope today is kind to you. 🤍
